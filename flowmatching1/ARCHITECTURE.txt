Effect:
 L_flow:     drives cells toward B  zier path
 L_geom:     keeps cells at rest in stable regions  
 L_context:  enforces consistency across conditions  
 L_smooth:   prevents Jacobian collapse  

Tuning strategy:
  1. Keep1.0 flow 
  4. Increase   3. Increase   2. Increase
